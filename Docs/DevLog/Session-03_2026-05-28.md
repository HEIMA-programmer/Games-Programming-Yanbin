## 2. What changed since last session

- **Added (Ride Your Echo, `dcfb0f2`):** the echo clone gains a child `Standpoint` — a
  `BoxCollider2D` + one-way `PlatformEffector2D` (surfaceArc 170°) on the Ground layer.
  You can now jump onto your clone from above and stand on it; jumping up through it from
  below passes through. Riders are carried by the clone's per-frame delta via a
  `Physics2D.OverlapBoxAll` strip above the surface (feet-height filtered), so side or
  bottom touches never drag the player. `BeginDissolve` disables the standpoint so a
  dissolving echo can't carry you away. The root trigger circle is untouched — plates
  still fire.
- **Added (art baseline, `8518e3e` + `22b516d`):** sprites switch from procedural shapes to

  `EchoImportedAssetSettings` (an `AssetPostprocessor` forcing Point filter / per-folder

## 3. Decisions & how my thinking changed

- **Decision:** **Supersede the "100 %-procedural sprites" principle** and adopt a licensed
  1-Bit kit (with full credit in `CREDITS.md`).
  **Why:** the fresh-player verdict — "plays better than it looks" — is exactly the failure
  the Concept Document warned mattered for a vertical slice: presentation debt was taxing
  how seriously the *mechanics* were taken. Hand-drawing comparable pixel art myself is
  weeks I don't have; the procedural generators had hit their quality ceiling.
  **Alternatives considered:** keep everything procedural and spend M2 polishing sprites
  (rejected — wrong skill investment for this module); full colour art packs (rejected —
  a strict 1-Bit + cyan identity is *more* distinctive and hides the fact the assets are
  stock). **Honesty note:** the README's claim that all art and audio were original /
  procedurally generated ("original except one licensed font") is gone — replaced by
  accurate licensing credits. The generators stay in source as a documented fallback.
- **Decision:** carry riders with an **`OverlapBoxAll` strip check**, not collision events.
  **Why:** a one-way `PlatformEffector2D` still fires `OnCollisionEnter/Exit` during the
  visual pass-through, so event-driven carrying jitters and drags on side contact. Polling
  a thin strip above the standpoint and filtering by feet height gives clean "standing on
  top" semantics. **Alternatives:** parenting the player to the clone (rejected — breaks
  the player's own physics), velocity matching (rejected — drifts on vertical rides).
- **Decision:** treat **peer feedback as a scheduling input**, not just a bug list. Ride
  Your Echo was already on the backlog; the playtest didn't invent it, it *re-prioritised*
  it — a fresh player's expectation ("he's me, I'm solid") is evidence about which backlog
  item pays off first. Same logic pulled the art milestone forward.

## 4. Problems hit & how I solved them

- **Kit sprites rendered stretched, not tiled.** Cause: `SpriteDrawMode.Tiled` silently
  falls back to stretching when a sprite has a Tight mesh from its first import (the
  postprocessor only fires on new imports). Fix: `EchoSpriteSlicer` force-sets
  `SpriteMeshType.FullRect` on every sheet it slices.
- **Applying TMP title outlines spammed an edit-mode warning** ("Instantiating material
  during edit mode"). Fix: `ApplyOutline` explicitly instantiates `fontSharedMaterial`
  with `Object.Instantiate` and assigns it back — per-instance material, no warning.
- **Menu diorama bled through the modal overlays** (Playtest 03 obs #3). Fix: dim panel
  alpha 0.9 → 1.0.
- **One sheet (`Char_fire`) sliced on the wrong grid** — it is 32×32 where the other
  character sheets are 48×48; fixed by giving the slicer a per-sheet cell table instead of
  one global cell size.
