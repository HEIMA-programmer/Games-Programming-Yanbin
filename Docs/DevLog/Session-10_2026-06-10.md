## 3. Decisions & how my thinking changed

- **Decision (the big one): cut Level 3.**
  **Why:** L3 existed only as a retired blockout; everything it was designed to teach
  (stealth, decoys, composite puzzles) L2 now teaches better. Bringing it to the new bar
  would cost the entire feel/finish pass everywhere else — and the project's standing
  principle is *refinement over scale*: depth and polish over level count. The story is
  also simply **stronger** ending at 97 % — "one trace still missing… found you" — than
  with a third corridor between the player and the ending.
  **Alternatives considered:** rush L3 to parity overnight (rejected — it would dilute
  exactly the polish that distinguishes the build); ship it as a visible blockout
  (rejected — it would undercut the quality bar everywhere else). The scene and its
  builder stay in the repo as honest history.
- **Decision:** the ending is **cutscenes + soundtrack**, not a new mechanic.
  **Why:** Playtest 04's closing note — "give it an ending; and music, it feels like a
  demo" — matched my own list. The fragments had been *pointing at* a story for three
  sessions; the acts pay it off. Captions reuse the terminal's exact grammar so the
  cutscenes feel like the same machine speaking.
- **Decision:** **license the soundtrack** rather than extend the procedural audio, and
  disclose the AI-generated cutscene images in `CREDITS.md`.
  **Why:** the music-box/chiptune split *is* the story's two voices; the procedural pads
  could never carry the Träumerei ending. Same honesty policy as the Session 03 art
  decision: use licensed/generated assets openly, credit them precisely (per-track licence
  election documented), keep original work where it is genuinely mine (all SFX remain
  synthesized).
- **Decision:** music transitions go **through silence**, never crossfade.
  **Why:** the tracks are in different keys and different sound-families by design — a
  crossfade smears them. Drift out under the fade-to-black, one breath of silence, bloom
  in. The curve shapes (squared out, squared in) keep both tails perceptually even.
- **Decision:** keep an **adversarial review pass** on my own new audio code before
  merging. It found two real bugs (a pending-bloom leak when skipping during the silence
  gap, and a dead fader method that could soft-lock scene loads). On a 9-hour overnight
  push, the cheap hour of self-review caught what playtesting wouldn't have hit until the
  demo.

## 4. Problems hit & how I solved them

- **Infinite wall-climb (Playtest 04 #1).** *Cause:* the ground-check box (0.72u) was
  wider than the player capsule (0.62u), and walls share the Ground layer — hugging a wall
  refilled coyote time every frame, so jumps chained forever. *Fix:* ground check narrowed
  to 0.5u **plus** a frictionless physics material on the capsule, so it slides off faces
  instead of clinging. (Two causes, two fixes — either alone leaves an exploit.)
- **Sentinel jump-over (Playtest 04 #2).** *Fix:* scene geometry only — a ceiling massif
  over the gaze-sentinel zone, mirroring the duct's. No code pretends to solve a layout
  problem.
- **Crate trains stalled (Playtest 04 #3).** *Cause:* the pusher treated *any* BoxCast hit
  as a wall and clamped its move — a neighbouring crate read as a wall. *Fix:*
  `ChainPush` hand-over: the lead crate passes the wanted delta down the train each tick
  (full push speed, ramping one frame per link); opposite shoves cancel; a replaying ghost
  crate displaces live crates out of its path and depenetrates any squeezed overlap when
  the echo ends. The recorder needed no changes — position playback composes cleanly.
- **Scene-load flash (Playtest 04 #6).** *Cause #1:* the first rendered frame used the
  camera's serialized editor pose. *Fix:* `CameraFollow.OnEnable → SnapToTarget()` (clamped
  target, zeroed velocity). *Cause #2:* the post-load hitch frame's huge delta swallowed
  the fade-in. *Fix:* the fader holds black for two frames after load and clamps fade
  deltas to 1/30 s (the same clamp the music fades got).
- **L0 had no HUD/pause/victory UI (Playtest 04 #4).** *Cause:* the hand-authored L0
  never received the GameplayCanvas the builder used to create. *Fix:* canvas re-added,
  GameManager's six UI references rewired.
- **Exit doors were silent (Playtest 04 #5).** *Cause:* the `ExitDoor` prefab shipped with
  empty clip slots, and the scene-built doors lacked AudioSources entirely. *Fix:* clips
  wired in the prefab (`doorslide` / `doorclose`), and an AudioSource + clips wired on the
  L1 scene door (L0's was already wired). **Known gap, found during the
  documentation pass:** the L2 scene door — the one that ends the game — was *missed*; it
  still has no AudioSource or clips and remains silent. Logged on the presentation-prep
  fix list (see [Playtest 05](../PlayTestNotes/Playtest-05_2026-06-11.md)).
- **Found in self-review:** skipping a cutscene during the silence gap let the *previous*
  scene's track bloom into the next scene (drift now kills any in-flight transition
  first); a dead `FadeInNow()` method could `StopAllCoroutines` mid-load and leave the
  fader stuck `isFading` — every later scene load would silently no-op. Deleted.
