## 2. What changed since last session

- **Added (`PushableCrate`):** a kinematic crate (kit chest art at 2×) with hand-rolled
  gravity and side-push detection — walk into a side face while grounded and pushing toward
  it, and it slides at 2 u/s (a `BoxCast` against walls stops it; crate-pushes-crate chains
  are deliberately unsupported for now). Crates sit on the **Ground layer**, so they're
  standable/vaultable, and they carry a `PlateActivator` — a crate parked on a pressure
  plate holds it down *forever*.
- **Added (echo-recorded crates):** `EchoRecorder` snapshots every crate tick-for-tick
  alongside the player frames. On release, only the crates the player actually *disturbed*
  rewind to their recording-start pose and **replay their exact recorded path** with the
  clone, on the same tick index. When the clone dissolves, crates unlock at their replayed
  end pose — the puzzle result persists. If a rewinding crate would overlap the player, the
  player is gently nudged out sideways instead of being thrown by physics depenetration.
- **Added (`ProximityMine`):** Idle → Fuse → Exploding → Cooldown. A player **or an echo
  clone** within 1.3u arms it; its idle blink accelerates (3 → 12 fps) through a visible
  0.8 s fuse; the 1.1u blast respawns the player / dissolves the clone; it vanishes for 3 s
  and re-arms. Crates never trigger it. Counterplay: bait-and-retreat, or spend an echo as
  a decoy — the decoy verb extends to a second enemy type for free.
- **Added:** `LiftPlatform` prefab — the kit platform strip on a ping-pong `MovingPlatform`
  (rises 3.4u at 2.5 u/s, 0.9 s dwell), docking flush with the floor for walk-on boarding.
- **Level 1 = SECTOR 01 — PLAYROOM**, hand-authored: a flat 34u corridor with minimal
  collision. Puzzle grammar separates the two mechanics: the far gate needs **both**

  corridor and the post-gate stretch. Guidance beats teach the asymmetry explicitly:
  *"heavy enough to hold a plate down FOREVER — a recording is not."*
- **Changed (L0):** two free-play crates added near the start, so pushing is *met* in a

## 3. Decisions & how my thinking changed

- **Decision:** crate replay is **pure position playback**, not re-simulation.
  **Why:** this is the determinism risk flagged back in Session 02 §6. If the clone
  re-*pushes* a simulated crate, any collision divergence compounds and the recorded
  solution can fail on playback — the one thing a recording mechanic must never do. Playing
  back recorded positions tick-for-tick **cannot** diverge, by construction. The cost
  (replaying crates are briefly "ghosts" that ignore live physics) is handled at the
  edges: nudge the player out of a rewinding crate, release crates at end pose.
  **Alternatives considered:** full re-simulation with deterministic pushes (rejected —
  unprovable under collisions with the live player); freezing crates during replay
  (rejected — kills the whole verb).
- **Decision:** crates are **kinematic with hand-rolled gravity**, not dynamic bodies.
  **Why:** dynamic crates get shoved by the player capsule, jitter on plate edges, and
  fight `MovePosition` playback. Kinematic + my own gravity/BoxCast keeps push speed
  authored (2 u/s), keeps plate triggers firing (`useFullKinematicContacts`), and makes
  recording/replay trivially exact.
- **Decision:** the L1 gate is a **provability argument, not a difficulty argument** — the
  chest *cannot* pass closed doors (so it must stay on A) and B is *unreachable* without
  the lift-ride + recording. Each mechanic is load-bearing; neither substitutes for the
  other. This is the level-design version of the M2 plan's one-fresh-rule-per-sector goal —
  both verbs must matter.
- **Decision:** mines target **player + clone but never crates** — the decoy verb
  generalises, the "world recording" verb stays clean (a crate is scenery to a mine).

## 4. Problems hit & how I solved them

- **A ping-pong `MovingPlatform` froze forever at its endpoint.**
  *Cause:* during the endpoint dwell the code parked `target = cur` while burning the wait
  timer; on the tick the timer expired, the arrival check was *still* true (target ==
  current position), so the direction flipped straight back — and the platform never left.
  Legacy builder-era code that had simply **never been play-tested** (the old levels never
  used ping-pong mode).
  *Fix:* the wait branch early-returns while the timer burns; the arrival flip can only
  fire on a real arrival.
- **The player sprite slowly slid back to the wrong height.**
  *Cause:* `PlayerAnimator`'s procedural bob lerped the visual's local Y toward a
  hard-coded `0`, silently erasing the authored feet-grounding offset from Session 07.
  *Fix:* cache the authored rest offset in `Awake` and oscillate around *it*.
- **A rewinding crate could materialise inside the player.** *Fix:* after rewind, if
  bounds intersect, reposition the player sideways to crate-extents + player-extents +
  0.05 — a deliberate "gentle squeeze-out" instead of letting the physics engine pick a
  violent depenetration.
