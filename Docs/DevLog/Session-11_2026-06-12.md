## 3. Decisions & how the thinking evolved

- **Deepen, don't widen.** The last gameplay hours went into an existing level (L1's
  two new segments exercise crates, mines, plates and the echo *together*) instead of
  new surface area — the same "depth over count" rule that cut Level 3.
- **Delete the fence.** The Legacy menu was a guard rail while hand-authoring was being
  proven; once Playtest 05 validated the ship build there was no reason to keep loaded
  builders pointed at hand-made scenes. Deletion is the honest end state — git history
  is the archive.
- **Teach at proof, not at position.** Moving the stun-coaching beat onto the
  `OnStunned` event closes the last "lesson before competence" hole found in playtests.
- **Fix the licence the day it is understood.** The CraftPix redistribution clause was
  honoured the same day it was read carefully, as a direct commit recorded in the
  changelog rather than hidden in a feature PR.

## 4. Problems hit & how I solved them

- **The previous story beat flashed through when the next one opened** — the terminal
  re-enabled its canvas before clearing the old text; cleared first, then faded.
- **The caption plate didn't render** — a sprite-less UI `Image` refuses to draw on
  that canvas; backed every tintable plate with the generated 8×8 white texture (the
  rule from Session 10 §4, now applied everywhere).
- **The stun window died under the beat** — the 2.5 s stun kept counting down during
  the blocking story beat that explains it; stun timers now respect `StoryFreeze`.
