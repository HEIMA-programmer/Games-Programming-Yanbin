## 2. What changed since last session

- **Added (pixel-perfect rendering):** `Aftertrace ▸ Art ▸ Configure Pixel Perfect Camera`
  adds URP's Pixel Perfect Camera (PPU 32, 640×360 reference — integer-scales ×2 to 720p,
  ×3 to 1080p; pixel snapping on). `Parallax` layers now snap to whole 1/32-unit positions
  to kill scroll shimmer. `Aftertrace ▸ Art ▸ Reimport All Imported Art @ PPU 32` force-fixes
  the straggler sheets that still carried stale PPU 48/16.
- **Changed (`CameraFollow`):** the clone pull-back zoom is now PPC-aware — with a PPC
  present it shifts the **reference resolution by whole integer zoom steps** instead of
  easing `orthographicSize` (which the PPC would overwrite every frame); `OnDisable`
  restores the base resolution so the camera can never get stuck zoomed out. Falls back to
  the old smooth easing when no PPC exists.
- **Added (animation):** `PlayerAnimator` gains optional frame sets — 3-frame run @ 12 fps,
  2-frame jump, 1-frame fall, static idle (kit `Char_Robot` sheet). While frames drive the
  look, the procedural squash/stretch eases back to neutral so it doesn't distort drawn
  pixels; with no frames assigned it stays fully procedural. Air states play once and hold.
- **Added (doors):** `Door` plays 4-frame open/close sprite animations (legacy slide kept
  as fallback when no frames are assigned). `AutoDoorExit` becomes a small **exit
  cinematic**: control freezes → door opens → the player alpha-fades into the doorway →
  door closes → beat → screen fades to the next scene. New `ExitDoor` prefab.
- **Changed (the pivot):** `EchoTilemap` (last session's code-painted terrain) is
  **deleted**; level builders now produce clean **gameplay blockouts** — colliders +
  entities + a flat grey `BlockoutFill` placeholder — and a new
  `Aftertrace ▸ Art ▸ Create Art Layer Skeleton` builds a correctly sorted `ArtGrid`
  (BG_Far / BG_Near / Terrain / Deco / Foreground, parallax pre-wired, Unlit material) for
  **hand-painting** in the Tile Palette. Full tile palettes + a `RockRule` rule-tile ship
  under `Assets/Art/Palettes/`. Level_00 carries the first hand-painted pass.
- **Added (editor safety):** every Build menu action now **confirms before overwriting a
  scene** (hand art is at stake); the destructive "Clean Generated Assets" is replaced by a
  narrow "Clean Orphaned Tile Cache". `EchoAudio` gains
  `Aftertrace ▸ Art ▸ Regenerate Audio Only`; door open/close SFX redesigned from a slide
  drone into short mechanical clicks.

## 3. Decisions & how my thinking changed

- **Decision (the big one):** environment art moves **procedural → hand-authored**, with
  the procedural pipeline retreating to what it is genuinely good at: *gameplay blockouts*,
  prefabs, UI scaffolding and audio.
  **Why:** two full sessions of code-painted terrain (04, 05) kept landing *near* the kit's
  showcase look but never *at* it — composition is a judgement call per screen, and code
  placement was always one rule short. The honest move is to stop spending sessions
  approximating hand-placement in code and just hand-place — on top of blockouts that stay
  deterministic and regenerable. **Alternatives considered:** more placement heuristics
  (rejected — diminishing returns demonstrated twice); keeping full procedural for some
  levels only (rejected — two art pipelines to maintain).
  **Trade-off accepted:** scenes stop being 100 % regenerable. Mitigation: builders now
  confirm before overwriting, and the blockout/art split means a rebuild loses paint, not
  design.
- **Decision:** clone pull-back zoom under PPC uses **discrete integer steps**.
  **Why:** smooth ortho-size easing and pixel-perfect rendering are mutually exclusive —
  the PPC recomputes the ortho size after `LateUpdate`. A one-step snap keeps the "you and
  your echo both on screen" intent while staying on the pixel grid; on windows too small to
  spare a step there is simply no pull-back. **Alternatives:** disable PPC during recording
  (rejected — visible shimmer pop), render-texture zoom (rejected — scope).
- **Decision (accepted timing change):** frame-animated doors toggle their blocking
  collider at animation **start** rather than physically sliding away over 0.3 s. A subtle
  gameplay-timing difference at thresholds — accepted and noted, since no puzzle times door
  collision that tightly.
- **Decision (accepted loss, temporary):** the blockout purge also removes the world-space
  narrative text panels, the floating "R" keycap, guide arrows, the menu diorama and the
  checkpoint flag visuals — the world goes *bare* this session. Wrong to ship, right to
  pass through: the replacement (a proper diegetic dialogue/terminal system over
  hand-authored scenes) is exactly what the next block of work builds. Logged so the gap is
  a decision, not an accident.

## 4. Problems hit & how I solved them

- **Clone zoom died the moment a PPC was added.** *Cause:* the PPC recomputes
  `orthographicSize` after `LateUpdate`, silently overwriting `CameraFollow`'s eased value.
  *Fix:* drive the PPC's reference resolution in integer zoom steps instead (§3).
- **Parallax layers shimmered against the pixel grid.** *Cause:* fractional sub-pixel layer
  positions. *Fix:* `Parallax.pixelSnap` rounds positions to 1/32-unit increments.
- **A few sheets still rendered at the wrong pixel density.** *Cause:* boss/trap/number
  sheets imported before the PPU unification kept stale meta (48/16). *Fix:* the
  `Reimport All @ PPU 32` sweep.
- **One stray menu click could wipe hand-painted art.** *Cause:* builders overwrote scenes
  silently, and "Clean Generated Assets" deleted sprites/prefabs/scenes wholesale. *Fix:*
  confirmation dialogs on every builder + the clean action narrowed to the tile cache only.
