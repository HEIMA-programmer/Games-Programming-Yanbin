## 2. What changed since last session

- **Changed (pixel scale):** `EchoImportedAssetSettings.PpuFor` collapsed from a dozen
  per-sheet rules (16/32/48/80) to a single `=> 32f`. With one PPU, a 48-px character is
  exactly 3× a 16-px tile — the kit's intended proportion. The ×1.4 visual-scale hacks on
  player / echo / drone / checkpoint are removed, and menu / atmosphere decorations are
  rescaled by the inverse PPU ratios (×0.4 for ex-80 sheets, ×0.667 ex-48, ×2 ex-16) so
  nothing changes world size.
- **Added (`EchoTilemap.cs`):** terrain is now a real Unity **Tilemap painted entirely from
  code** (`SetTile`) during Build All — no manual Tile Palette work. `PaintSolid` snaps each
  block to the 0.5u grid and lays the kit's rock tiles: crest indices {6,7,8} on the top
  row, dithered body {23,24,25} in the middle (varied by grid position), base {40,41,42} on
  the bottom. Tile assets are created idempotently under `Assets/Art/Tiles/` with
  `ColliderType.None` — **collision stays on the per-block `BoxCollider2D`**, so physics is
  unchanged by construction.
- **Added (`BuildBackgroundWall`):** a continuous dim facility wall behind the play space —
  80-px background tiles on a 2.5u grid (diagonal-line walls with pipes / panels / cables
  woven in, ~30 % black gaps), tinted to 45 % alpha, parallax factor 0.06. `BuildAtmosphere`
  keeps its planets/stars but dims and pushes them further back.
- **Changed:** the previous session's per-block decorators (`AddTopEdge`,
  `SprinkleCraters`) are no longer called by terrain — the kit's crest/base tiles do that
  job natively. `PlaceGroundProps` stays.
- **Changed (menu):** the huge backdrop planet behind the title becomes a 12 % alpha ghost
  (full white read as a noisy blob behind the title at the new scale).
- **Reverted:** the experimental Bayer-dithered collectible glow → a smaller, dimmer smooth
  aura (the sparse rim dots read as noise at halo size; comment in `EchoArt.cs` documents
  the revert and defers a "crisp sparkle" to a later pass).
- **Removed:** the 8 obsolete procedural sprite PNGs (`player`, `echo`, `drone`, `door`,
  `platform`, `fragment`, `endarch`, `moon`) — their keys are served by the imported-kit
  mapping; the generator code remains in source as the fallback.

## 3. Decisions & how my thinking changed

- **Decision:** paint Tilemaps **from code**, not by hand in the Tile Palette.
  **Why:** the kit is a tileset designed for Unity's Tilemap — my procedural sprite
  approximations (Session 04's tiled single-tile + white edge) kept falling short of the
  kit's own showcase shots. Painting tiles from code gets the kit's real composition *and*
  keeps the "Build All regenerates everything" workflow. **Alternatives considered:**
  hand-painting in the Tile Palette (rejected *this week* — it breaks one-click rebuild;
  noted as the likely end-state if procedural placement hits its own ceiling, which is
  exactly what happened the very next session).
- **Decision:** visuals and collision are now **fully decoupled** — tiles carry
  `ColliderType.None`; the blocks' `BoxCollider2D`s are untouched.
  **Why:** the art can iterate freely with a physics-regression risk of zero by
  construction. The PR's "gameplay is byte-identical" claim rests on this decoupling, not
  on play-testing every jump again.
- **Decision:** **defer the Pixel Perfect Camera** even though the unified PPU begs for it.
  **Why:** URP's PPC overrides the orthographic size and interacts with `CameraFollow`'s
  clone pull-back zoom — bolting it on at the end of an art session risks a framing
  regression I can't properly test tonight. Scheduled as the *opening* item of the next
  session instead.

## 4. Problems hit & how I solved them

- **One source pixel rendered at up to five on-screen sizes.** *Cause:* the original import
  rules chose PPU per sheet so that "one sprite ≈ one world unit" — convenient for layout,
  fatal for a unified pixel grid. *Fix:* PPU 32 everywhere + compensating local scales at
  the few places that relied on the old sizes (menu decorations, atmosphere planets).
- **Removing the ×1.4 character scale shifted feet off the ground.** *Cause:* the visual
  child's scale participated in where the sprite met the collider. *Fix:* re-grounded the
  visuals at scale 1.0; logged that the kit's *internal* bottom padding still makes sprites
  hover slightly — properly measured and fixed in the Level-0 rework (Session 07).
- **The dithered collectible glow read as noise** (carried experiment from Session 04).
  *Fix:* reverted to a smooth radial aura, smaller and dimmer.
