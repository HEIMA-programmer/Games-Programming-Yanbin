  ordered-dither matrix** instead of RNG.
- **Added (backgrounds):** `BuildAtmosphere` — two parallax layers (factor 0.1): 2–3 dim

## 3. Decisions & how my thinking changed

- **Decision:** state speaks through **brightness/alpha/shape, never hue**.
  **Why:** a 1-Bit identity collapses the moment one element cheats with colour — and the
  playtest showed colour was doing *no* communicative work that shape wasn't already doing
  (the plate physically depresses; the cone has a direction). Cyan stays reserved for the
  player/HUD accent layer only.
  **Honesty note:** "1-Bit" here is a design language, not a literal 2-colour framebuffer —
  button hover/press states are mid-greys and several elements are white-at-fractional-alpha
  over black. Perceptually monochrome; technically greyscale.
- **Decision:** make every decoration **deterministic** — craters seed `Random.InitState`
  from the block's position (and restore the previous RNG state); the new sprites use a
  Bayer matrix with no RNG at all.
  **Why:** Session 02's lesson (seeded WAVs) generalised: anything a rebuild regenerates
  must be byte-stable or git churn hides real changes. Build All twice → identical output.
- **Decision:** **neutralise** the 2D lights rather than delete them this session.
  **Why:** the unlit shader makes them no-ops, so deleting them is pure cleanup with scene
  churn across five scenes — deferred to the next builder pass rather than mixed into an
  art PR at 5 a.m. (Logged, not forgotten.)
- **Decision (accepted loss):** deleting the cluttered backdrops also deletes the L1
  background **NPC silhouettes** — the wordless "people worked here once" device. Accepted
  because the clutter was hurting readability more than the silhouettes were adding story;
  the narrative job moves to a proper diegetic system later (it eventually became the
  Session-07 terminal).

## 4. Problems hit & how I solved them

- **Player couldn't jump off a rising platform** (found in Session 03's end-to-end pass;
  most visible on the L3 plate-elevator).
  *Cause:* `MovingPlatform` carried riders with `rider.MovePosition(rider.position + delta)`
  every `FixedUpdate` — `MovePosition` on the rider overwrote the rider's own upward jump
  velocity each physics step, re-gluing them to the platform.
  *Fix:* carry via a direct position write (`rider.position += delta`), which preserves the
  rider's velocity, **plus** an early-out that releases any rider already moving up faster
  than 4 u/s (they've jumped — stop carrying them). Both halves matter; the threshold
  prevents the carry re-capturing the first jump frame.
- **The first dither experiments read as noise at gameplay size.** A 32×32 crater with a
  fully dithered rim looked like compression artefacts at PPU 32. Fix: dither only the rim
  transition band (threshold < 6 on the Bayer matrix), keep the cavity solid, and bias
  craters toward the lower half of blocks where the eye expects shadow.
