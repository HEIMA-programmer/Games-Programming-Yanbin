# Playtest 04 — 2026-06-10 (evening)

**Build / version:** v0.9.1 (after Session 09 — first build where L0, L1 and L2 all exist
in their final designs)  **Levels tested:** MainMenu + Level_00 → Level_02
**Tester:** the same coursemate as [Playtest 03](Playtest-03_2026-05-28.md) (so: has seen
the *old* game two weeks ago, but none of the rebuilt levels), plus a partial self-run after.
**Setup:** my machine, Unity 2022.3 editor Play mode, keyboard.

## Goal of this round

The last fresh-eyes round before the final overnight push. Three questions:

1. Do the rebuilt levels **teach themselves** — echo gate, crates, mines, decoy-stun,
   step-stool — with only the terminal beats as guidance?
2. Can the levels be **broken**? (Deliberate exploit hunt — I asked the tester to cheat
   wherever possible.)
3. What does the build still lack to feel *finished* rather than *functional*?

## Tasks given to the tester

1. Fresh run, menu → L0 → L1 → L2, thinking aloud. No help unless hard-stuck.
2. Second pass: "now try to break it — skip puzzles, climb things, cheese the drones."
3. Closing question: "what feels missing?"

## What I changed because of this

All landed the same night in PR #153 (commit `5c0853a`) — see
[Session 10](../DevLog/Session-10_2026-06-10.md):

- **Obs #1** → root-caused the infinite wall-climb: the ground-check box (0.72u) was wider
  than the capsule (0.62u) and walls share the Ground layer, so hugging a wall refilled
  coyote time every frame. Ground check narrowed to 0.5u **and** a frictionless physics
  material on the capsule so it slides off faces instead of clinging.
- **Obs #2** → a ceiling massif (`StealthCeilingS5`) sealed the jump-over above the
  sentinel zone — same treatment the duct already had.
- **Obs #3** → crates now **chain-push as a train** (Sokoban-style): full-speed shove
  hand-over between neighbours; a replaying ghost crate displaces live crates and
  depenetrates them when the echo ends.
- **Obs #4** → L0 regained its GameplayCanvas — fragment counter, Esc pause, victory
  panel — with the GameManager's UI references rewired.
- **Obs #5** → the L1 exit door got its missing AudioSource + open/close clips wired in
  the scene, and the `ExitDoor` prefab's empty clip slots were patched at the source.
  *(Correction, added during the v1.0.0 validation pass: the L2 scene door was missed and
  is still silent — see [Playtest 05](Playtest-05_2026-06-11.md).)*
- **Obs #6** → camera now snaps to its clamped target on enable, and the scene fader holds
  two black frames after load + clamps its fade delta, so no wrong-pose frame can show.
- **Obs #7** → menu button spacing 85 → 110 px (buttons are 92 px tall — the old layout
  overlapped by definition).
- **Obs #10** → became the shape of Session 10 itself: the story ending (cutscene acts) and
  a licensed soundtrack, and with them the decision to **cut Level 3** and end the story
  after L2.
