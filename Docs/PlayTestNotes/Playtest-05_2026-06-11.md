# Playtest 05 — 2026-06-11 (dawn)

**Build / version:** v1.0.0 (the presentation build, after PR #153)
**Levels tested:** full game flow — MainMenu → Cut_00 → Level_00 → Cut_01 → Level_01 →
Cut_02 → Level_02 → Cut_03 → MainMenu, plus Level Select replays.
**Tester:** self (developer) — this is the ship-build **validation run**, not a discovery
round: every Playtest 04 observation gets re-tested on the final build, plus the systems
that didn't exist in that build (cutscenes, soundtrack).
**Setup:** Unity 2022.3 editor Play mode, keyboard, 1080p game view.

## Goal of this round

1. **Every Playtest 04 issue is verifiably fixed** on the build that will be presented.
2. The **new systems** (cutscene acts, drift-through-silence music, completion flag) work
   across the full flow, including the abuse cases (skipping at bad moments).
3. Nothing in the cut-Level-3 de-wiring left a dangling path.

## Tasks performed

1. Full fresh run, menu to "thank you for playing", letting every cutscene play out.
2. Second run skipping aggressively: Esc through every act, including **during the silence
   gap** of a music transition (the case that leaked before).
3. Exploit re-test at every wall the Playtest 04 tester climbed: hug + mash jump.
4. L2 segment 5: attempt the sentinel jump-over from every ledge.
5. L1: two-crate train pushes from both sides; record a crate-push, stand in the ghost's
   path, let the echo end while crates overlap.
6. L0: Esc pause (resume / restart / menu), fragment counter, victory panel.
7. Watch the first frame of every scene transition for the camera flash; listen to every
   exit door.
8. Level Select: jump straight into each level (bypassing cutscenes), finish, confirm
   return paths; confirm no Level 3 entry anywhere (menu, build, progression).
9. Completion: confirm Act 4 sets the completed flag and the menu shows its completion
   state.

## What I changed because of this

Almost nothing — which is the point of a validation run. Seven of the eight Playtest 04
observations are verifiably closed on the shipping build; the run surfaced **one residual
defect** and one documentation debt:

- **Obs #5 (L2 exit door still silent)** → logged on the presentation-prep fix list, and
  the Session 10 / Playtest 04 write-ups corrected so they don't claim it fixed. It's a
  two-minute scene fix (add AudioSource, assign `doorslide`/`doorclose`) — deliberately
  *not* hot-patched at dawn before the presentation without a re-run.
- **Obs #10** → the documentation backfill scheduled in
  [Session 10 §6](../DevLog/Session-10_2026-06-10.md#6-next-session-plan): dev-log sessions
  for the rebuild sprints, this playtest series, and a README rewritten to the shipped
  reality.
