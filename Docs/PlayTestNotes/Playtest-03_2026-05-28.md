# Playtest 03 — 2026-05-28

**Build / version:** v0.5.1 (end of Session 02)  **Levels tested:** Level_00 – Level_03
**Tester:** a coursemate (first time seeing the game; plays platformers regularly)
**Setup:** my machine, Unity 2022.3 editor Play mode, keyboard. I sat behind and took notes;
I answered questions only after they had tried something twice.

## Goal of this round

This is the **fresh-player round owed since Session 01** (carried over twice). Three questions:

1. Is the record mechanic (**hold R**) understood from the world-space prompt alone, without
   me explaining anything?
2. Does the L3 stealth corridor read as *"hide, time it, or send a decoy"* to someone seeing
   it cold?
3. What does a fresh pair of eyes snag on that I've gone blind to after three days of
   intensive building? *(Erratum 2026-06-13: this originally said "two weeks" — the
   build was 3 days old at this point, the concept document 9 days.)*

## Tasks given to the tester

1. Play from the main menu through L0 → L3 in order. No instructions from me beyond
   "it's keyboard + mouse".
2. Think aloud — especially say out loud what you *expect* to happen before you try things.
3. Afterwards: "what would you change first?"

## Observations

| # | Where | What happened | Severity |
| - | ----- | ------------- | -------- |
| 1 | L1, first plate puzzle | **Tried to jump on the echo's head — twice.** Said "can I stand on him?", jumped onto the clone, fell straight through, looked genuinely confused. Expected the clone to be solid from above. | Med (expectation gap) |
| 2 | Everywhere | "It plays better than it looks." Read the flat procedural shapes as *placeholder art*: couldn't always tell decorative rectangles from solid platforms at a glance, and said the game "looks like a tech demo" even though the mechanics felt finished. | High |
| 3 | Main menu | Opened **How to Play** — the menu diorama bled through the semi-transparent panel and made the text hard to read. | Med |
| 4 | L2/L3, after deaths | Didn't know where respawn would put them — checkpoints are invisible triggers, so every death was a small surprise. | Med |
| 5 | HUD | Misread the three-diamond fragment row as a **health bar** ("I thought I had three lives"). | Med |
| 6 | L1, record prompt | Understood **R** from the world-space keycap prompt on the second try (first recording was wasted standing still). Acceptable — no change. | Low |
| 7 | L3 corridor | Stealth corridor read correctly: hid behind the first pillar, then chose the decoy on their own. **Resolved — the Session 02 rework holds up with a fresh player.** | — |

## Tester comments (in their words)

> "Wait, can I stand on him? … oh, I fall through. That feels like it *should* work —
> he's me, I'm solid."

> "It plays better than it looks. The white boxes scream prototype. If this looked like a
> real game I'd have taken the puzzles more seriously from the start."

> "The recording thing is cool once it clicks. The drone corridor was my favourite bit —
> I felt clever sending the ghost the wrong way."

## What I changed because of this

This round ran the same evening, immediately before the Session 03 build, and
**re-ordered the Milestone 2 plan**
(see [Session 03 §1](../DevLog/Session-03_2026-05-28.md#1-plan-for-this-session)):

- **Obs #1** → *Ride Your Echo* was already a Milestone 2 backlog candidate; this promoted
  it from "one of four options" to the **first verb built**. The clone now has a one-way
  standable top surface — you can jump on your echo's head and be carried with it.
  Commit `dcfb0f2` (PR #146).
- **Obs #2** → pulled the art-identity work **forward from Milestone 3**: switched the
  sprite baseline to the licensed CraftPix 1-Bit kit with a black-background monochrome
  identity, instead of spending M2 purely on mechanics. Commits `8518e3e` + `22b516d`
  (PR #146).
- **Obs #3** → How to Play / Level Select dim panels made fully opaque (alpha 0.9 → 1.0).
  Commit `e77053a` (PR #146).
- **Obs #4** → checkpoints got a visible flag-pole sprite + a small light. Commit `22b516d`
  (PR #146).
- **Obs #5** → HUD fragment row replaced by a single gem icon + a "0 / 3" counter, so it
  reads as a *count*, not a resource. Commit `22b516d` (PR #146).

## Resolved after this round

- **R is learnable without being told** (one wasted recording is fine — the wasted try *is*
  the lesson). Keeping the keycap prompt as the only tutorialisation for now.
- **The L3 stealth corridor reads correctly** to a fresh player — both the hide option and
  the decoy option were discovered unaided.

## Still open / next round

- The art switch this feedback triggered is a *baseline*, not a finished look — next rounds
  need to re-check readability once the world is genuinely monochrome.
- *Ride Your Echo* shipped as an **optional** ability. Watch whether testers actually
  discover it when no puzzle requires it (suspect: they won't — flagged for the level
  redesign).
